# My app: Court Check

**Domain:** Pickup basketball at UW intramural (IMA) gyms. Players who are at a gym post quick updates: how many people are there, whether a game needs players, and a short note.

**User story:** As a UW student who plays pickup basketball, I need to see how crowded each intramural gym is right now and whether any game needs players, so that I go to the gym where I'll actually get to play instead of waiting on the sideline.

**What the user types or picks:**
- Picks a gym (or "All gyms") and how far back to look (last 1, 2, or 6 hours) to see the latest status and recent reports.
- At the gym, picks their name and the gym, types how many people are there, checks "need players", adds an optional note, and posts it.
